## 4. How to outrank the OTAs (playbook)

1. **Own the long‑tail, not the head term.** Win "maría chiquita beachfront rental", "bala beach panama rental", "casa playa colón frente al mar" — OTAs have thin pages there; we have a deep, fast, schema‑rich one.
2. **Per‑language indexable pages** (already built: `/en /es /de /pt /fr`, hreflang, canonical, JSON‑LD `VacationRental`+`FAQPage`). This is why we rank per language while OTAs serve one geo page.
3. **Google Business Profile** for the property (category: Vacation home rental, location María Chiquita) → wins the local pack the OTAs can't.
4. **Backlinks/citations:** list the new domain on local PA directories, Portobelo dive shops, "things to do Colón" blogs, and from your IG/TikTok bios. Even 10–20 relevant links beat a brand‑new domain's cold start.
5. **Area‑guide content** (Tier 2 keywords) → internal links to the booking page; this is what aggregators can't replicate at the property level.
6. **Reviews + EEAT:** real photos, host bio/author, real reviews with `Review` schema, NAP consistency.
7. **Core Web Vitals:** convert photos to WebP/AVIF + `srcset`, compress the tour video to ~720p + poster (see DESIGNER‑BRIEF P1). Fast site = ranking + conversion edge over heavy OTA pages.
8. **Disambiguate from Panama City Beach, FL** in every English title/meta: always pair "Panama" with "Colón" or "Caribbean".

## 5. One‑line migration when you register the domain

The generator is already wired for it. In `build/content.py`:
```python
"custom_domain": "colonbeachrentals.net",   # was ""
```
Then `python3 build/generate.py` and push. It will:
- emit a `CNAME` file (GitHub Pages custom domain),
- switch all asset/link paths to root‑relative,
- rewrite `canonical`, `hreflang`, `og:url` and `sitemap.xml` to the apex domain.

DNS at the registrar (point to GitHub Pages):
```
A     @   185.199.108.153
A     @   185.199.109.153
A     @   185.199.110.153
A     @   185.199.111.153
CNAME www hermes08.github.io
```
Then in the repo: Settings → Pages → Custom domain = your domain, Enforce HTTPS. Finally submit the new `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and request indexing.
